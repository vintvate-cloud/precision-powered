- Carry the revised palette, typography, buttons, product surfaces, header, and footer across category, shop, product, and contact pages.
- Preserve existing product data, source images, routes, forms, and motion behavior; tune motion to match the reference and reduced-motion preferences.
- Verify desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, navigation, and page stability.
